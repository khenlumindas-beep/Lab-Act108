# SmartTrack — Faculty User Orientation (Phase 10)

Audience: faculty and staff who travel or apply for international mobility
support. Short read (~10 minutes). Administrators: see ADMIN_TRAINING.md.

## Why this system

SmartTrack keeps one record of your foreign travel: the request, the required
documents, compliance status, post-travel reports, and re-entry activities —
with notifications so nothing is a surprise.

## 1. Log in

1. Open the SmartTrack site (campus link or `flutter-web/index.html`).
2. Your campus email + password → **Sign In**.
3. First time? Change your password via the avatar menu → **Profile**.

If login fails repeatedly, contact OIGE — do not keep retrying with old passwords.

## 2. Your dashboard

The left rail is your home base: **Dashboard · Travels · Documents · Plans · More**.

- **Dashboard**: your travels at a glance — status chips (Upcoming, Submitted,
  Approved…), compliance hints, and quick stats.
- **Notifications** (bell): new remarks, status changes, announcements, reminders.

## 3. Create a travel request (FR-F02)

1. **Travels → New Travel** (or the add button on the travel list).
2. Fill in: event title, mobility type (Conference/Training/Meeting…),
   participant type, dates, location, country, college.
3. Save. Your request starts as **Submitted** and appears in OIGE’s review queue.
4. Track its workflow status on the travel card. You’ll be notified on every
   change; remarks from OIGE appear on the travel and in your notification feed.

## 4. Upload required documents (FR-F03/F06)

1. Open your travel → **Documents**.
2. Upload each required item (invitation letter, travel authority, passport
   copy, proof of funding, etc.). PDF/JPG/PNG are fine; 50 MB max per file.
3. **AI tip:** after upload the system suggests the document type, shows a
   confidence score, and may flag issues (e.g. “filename looks like a draft”,
   “file too small — placeholder?”, type mismatch). This is advice only —
   confirm the type is right.
4. Replace rejected files promptly; rejection reasons come as notifications and
   remarks.

## 5. Post-travel & re-entry (after you return)

- **Post-travel**: upload your travel report and any evidence OIGE requires.
- **Re-entry**: complete your re-entry actions (dissemination talk, MOV,
  photos, activity report) and upload proof.
- Missing items turn yellow/red on your compliance status and generate
  reminders — finishing them early clears the flags.

## 6. Check your compliance (FR-F07)

Open a travel → compliance snapshot:

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Fully Compliant** (green) | All required pre-travel documents verified | Nothing — keep originals safe |
| **Partially Compliant** (yellow) | Some items pending | Upload or fix the listed items |
| **Needs Action** (red) | Overdue or critical item missing/rejected | Act now; contact OIGE if blocked |

## 7. Remarks

OIGE remarks (approvals, rejection reasons, AI-drafted suggestions they chose
to send) are visible on the travel and under **More → Remarks (mine)**.
Remarks that start with `[AI suggested]` were drafted by the system and sent by
an administrator — the content has been reviewed by staff.

## 8. Your mobility plan (FR-A10)

**Plans** lets you view/submit your yearly mobility plan. Keep it current —
it feeds OIGE’s planning reports.

## 9. Tips

- Use a clear filename (`invitation_letter_jpayla.pdf`) — it helps both the AI
  checker and OIGE find things fast.
- Upload early; verification takes time before deadlines.
- Watch notifications — most delays happen because a replacement document was
  never seen.
- Questions: contact OIGE (admin) — they see the same record you do.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Can’t log in | Confirm campus email; ask OIGE to check account status |
| Upload fails | File type/size limits (PDF/JPG/PNG, ≤50 MB); try re-exporting |
| Status not changing | Status changes on OIGE review — check notifications/remarks |
| Need an old record | **Travels** list with filters; history is kept across years |
